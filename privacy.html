Water Logged — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to Water Logged (Android package com.taescience.waterlogged), a water-intake tracking app for Android phones and Wear OS watches. It explains what data the app collects, how it uses that data, and how it shares it. In short: your water data stays on your devices and in Health Connect. Water Logged has no server, no account system, no advertising, and no analytics, and it does not sell or share your data with third parties.

Data the app collects

Water Logged collects only the data needed to track water intake:

  • Hydration records: the amount of water you log (in millilitres/ounces) and the time you logged it.
  • Your daily goal: the daily water goal you set in Settings (16–200 oz).
  • App settings you choose: reminder interval and window, your default watch-tap amount, your complication-tap choice (add water or open the watch app), and your custom quick-add button amounts.

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not create an account for you. You do not sign in to use it.

Health Connect

With your permission, Water Logged reads and writes hydration data through Health Connect on your phone:

  • When you log water on the phone, the app writes a HydrationRecord (amount and time) to Health Connect.
  • When you log water on the watch, the phone companion writes that record to Health Connect once the watch syncs with the phone.
  • The app reads Health Connect hydration records to show your total for today, your streak, and your history (today, week, month, and year views).
  • Your daily goal is not stored in Health Connect; it lives only in the app's local settings, because Health Connect has no hydration-goal API.

Health Connect data is stored by the Android system, not by Water Logged, and is governed by your device's Health Connect permissions. You can review or revoke Health Connect access at any time in your device settings.

Phone–watch sync

If you use the Wear OS companion, the watch and phone exchange your water data (amounts, times, goal, and settings) directly between your own devices using Google Play services' Wearable Data Layer. This sync is device-to-device. Water Logged sends this data to no server of its own, because it operates none.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to provide the app's features: showing today's total and progress toward your goal, your streak and history, reminders to drink water, the watch Tile and complication, and the home-screen widget, Quick Settings Tile, and launcher shortcut. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Sharing

Water Logged does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, and it does not share data with advertisers or data brokers. The only places your data goes are: (1) Health Connect on your phone, as described above, under your control; and (2) your own paired watch, via the device-to-device sync described above.

Storage and retention

Your goal and settings are stored locally on your devices (phone preferences and watch preferences). Hydration records are stored in Health Connect. Data remains until you delete it as described below. There is no cloud backup provided by Water Logged.

Deleting your data

  • Clear today's log: in the phone app's Settings, "Clear today's log" deletes today's hydration records from Health Connect.
  • Undo a phone add: the "Undo" action on the confirmation shown after a phone add deletes phone adds made in the last 10 seconds.
  • Watch taps: once a watch tap has synced to Health Connect, it cannot be undone from inside Water Logged. Delete such records in the Health Connect app / your device's Health Connect settings.
  • Uninstalling: uninstalling Water Logged deletes its local settings, but it does not delete your Health Connect history. To delete that history, delete the records in Health Connect as above.

Security

Because data is stored locally and in Health Connect rather than on a server, its security depends in part on your device's lock screen and system protections. Water Logged does not transmit your data over the internet to any developer-operated service.

Children

Water Logged is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It collects only the hydration and settings data described above, on the user's own device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes to how data is collected, used, or shared will be reflected in this page before they take effect in the app.
